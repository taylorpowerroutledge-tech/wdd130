Join Us in a Journey of a Life Time

Family Adventure Package

Enjoy a relaxing day on the water with our Family Adventure Package! This low-intensity rafting experience is perfect for families looking to spend time together, enjoy beautiful scenery, and have some fun on the river without the challenge of intense rapids. Our experienced guides will help make the trip comfortable and enjoyable for everyone. Paddle together, take in the views, and create lasting family memories along the way. This is a great choice for families and first-time rafters looking for an easygoing outdoor adventure. Attendees must be 5 yrs and older.

Guide-Your-Own Package

Ready to take control of your adventure? Our Guide Your Own Package gives experienced rafters the freedom to explore the river at their own pace. Choose your route, work together as a team, and enjoy the excitement of navigating the water on your own. This package is designed for groups looking for a little more independence and adventure while enjoying the beautiful scenery around them. Gather your friends, grab your paddles, and make your rafting experience your own!

Intensity Adventure Package

Looking for an exciting challenge on the water? Our Intensity Adventure Package is designed for rafters who want a more thrilling experience. Take on faster-moving water, exciting rapids, and challenging sections of the river while working together as a team. With experienced guides helping you along the way, you'll experience the excitement of white water rafting while taking in the beautiful scenery. Bring your energy, teamwork, and sense of adventure for an unforgettable day on the river!
